About Nikki's Curry & All

Welcome to Nikki's Curry & All, where we bring the heart of Durban's authentic Indian cuisine right to your table in Kempton Park. Our journey started with a simple passion: to share the rich, vibrant, and unforgettable flavours of traditional home-cooked curries.

"From my home to yours."

This isn't just a slogan for us; it's our promise. Every dish is prepared with the same love, care, and attention to detail that you would find in our own family kitchen. We use only the freshest ingredients and time-honoured recipes passed down through generations to create meals that are bursting with flavour and warmth.

Whether you're craving a classic lamb curry, a creamy butter chicken, or our famous samoosas, we invite you to experience a taste of home with us.
